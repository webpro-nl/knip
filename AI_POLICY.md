# AI Contribution Policy

AI tools are welcome when contributing to Knip. Code, tests, and documentation
may all be generated, as long as the person behind the contribution understands
it and takes responsibility for it.

## You own what you submit

- Understand every change you submit, and be able to explain it during review.
- Verify it yourself: reproduce the issue and run the relevant tests locally.
- Prefer typing descriptions, and especially follow-up comments, yourself.
  Generated text is fine when, for example, a language barrier gets in the way.
- Keep descriptions and comments concise. No "wall of text".
- Stay involved through review, and answer for everything posted on your
  behalf.
- Follow the [contribution guidelines][1].

AI usage doesn't need to be disclosed.

## Not accepted

- Unreviewed AI output, or changes nobody can explain.
- Issues and pull requests opened by agents or bots without a person who
  reviewed them and stands behind them.
- Reports or fixes for problems that weren't reproduced.

Even so, generated pull requests and slop may be closed without explanation.
